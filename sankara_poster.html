10 / Why he was targeted
10
IMF
WITHIN WEEKS,
THE IMF
RETURNED.
The new government reversed everything: privatisation, structural adjustment, donor dependency. The world the IMF wanted. Cotton prices kept falling. The debt kept growing. Burkina Faso ranked among the world's poorest countries — again.

"You cannot carry out fundamental change
without a certain amount of madness."

— Thomas Sankara
He was killed because he was proof that another way was possible — that a poor country could feed itself, heal itself, educate itself, and refuse.
Source: World Bank structural adjustment records; UNCTAD; Harsch (2014);
Burkina Faso post-1987 economic data — IMF Article IV reports
